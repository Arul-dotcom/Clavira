Design Framework

The Compass That
Guided the Experience

Four core UX pillars engineered to remove cognitive load and establish clinical trust across every interaction.

Trust at the Core

Prominently displays CAP and NABL accreditation badges on auth and checkout screens.

Empowered Family Booking

Multi-patient carting lets users assign tests to dependents directly within a single checkout flow, cutting booking loops significantly.

Inclusive Accessibility

High-contrast typography and clear visual hierarchy designed for all age groups.

 Clarity in Every Report

Replaces numeric tables with visual range sliders and plain-English AI summary flags for instant visual.
